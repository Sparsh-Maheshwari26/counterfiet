About and safety

Reference data: 500 openFDA NDC Directory listings plus 12 fictional demo rows. openFDA is US-focused and is not an Indian approval database.

  • A match does not prove a physical pack is genuine.
  • No match does not mean a medicine is fake.
  • Batch numbers are not checked with manufacturers, and recalls are not checked.
  • Always confirm with a pharmacist or the manufacturer. In India, see CDSCO notices.
